# EqualTune 1.7

## New
- **Device recovery:** unplug or reconnect headphones (or switch a Bluetooth device) while routing and EqualTune picks the sound back up on its own.
- **Layout fits any window size:** the top bar and pages adapt as you resize, without wrapping or overlapping.
- **Manual preamp:** a preamp slider with a Reset button that fades smoothly, a clipping warning, and the preamp is saved with presets and read from `Preamp:` lines on import.
- **Import preview:** see how closely an imported curve matches EqualTune's 12 bands before you apply it. Also imports Room EQ Wizard filter settings, AutoEq GraphicEQ files and CSV or text frequency/gain tables.
- **Optional preset packs:** 100 presets to download from equaltune.app (37 headphones, 33 earphones, 30 genres). Based on AutoEq results, see the licence file in the download.
- Top-bar buttons now keep the same size and spacing in every vertical-bar skin, with Support staying top right.

## Notes
- Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The installer is not code-signed, so choose More info, then Run anyway.
