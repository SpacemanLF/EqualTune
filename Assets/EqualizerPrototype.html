Welcome to EqualTune

EqualTune shapes the sound of everything your PC plays — music, games, videos and calls — with a live equalizer. A quick setup gets you listening in under a minute.

  • Check that VB-CABLE (the free virtual audio device EqualTune routes sound through) is installed
  • Pick the headphones or speakers you listen on
  • Choose how EqualTune starts
